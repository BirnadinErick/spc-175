Archive

The College Archive is meant for safeguarding all the important and old documents of the College. It is located in the Matthews Block adjacent to the library. The Archive contains more than a hundred years old documents such as the attendance registers, results records and other various details of the past students and teachers.

Auditorium

The College Auditorium, generally known as Matthews’ Hall is located at the top floor of the Matthews’ Block. I can house more than 1500 persons.All the important events such as the Annual Prize Day, Parents’ Day, Annual Day celebrations of all the clubs and unions, Teachers’ Day etc. take place there.

Balling Machines for Cricket and Tennis

Balling Machines for cricket and tennis practice are available at the College. The College is very grateful for the generous donor of these modern gadgets which are very helpful to our students.

Basketball Court

A well maintained basketball court is found next to the College chapel. This was constructed in 2011. The entire project was sponsored by Brig. David and his spouse Perly. The College Basketball team is really lucky to have this court for their daily practice.

Board Room

An air-conditioned and well-furnished Board Room is located at the ground floor of the Technology Centre. It is very useful for having conferences and seminars. Thanks to our Loyal Patrician Mr. Arulananthan and his spouse from the UK for their generous contribution towards the creation of the Board Room.

Bus

A new bus for the College was donated by generous Old Patricians of the 1990 A/L Batch when Rev. Fr. M. Jero Selvanayagam was the Rector of the College. The bus is very useful for the College to take students for various competitions, excursions and for also teachers’ picnics etc. The College Bus is a real blessing to the whole Patrician Community.

Canteens

There are two canteens at the College serving the needs of the teachers and students. They are open during the interval and provide the teachers and students with nutritious food items and beverages.

Chapel

The College Chapel is one of the oldest buildings which stands to remind us of the glory of Greater St. Patrick’s. The Chapel is not simply a facility of the College but the source of God’s grace through which the daily events of the College receive strength to be accomplished. The daily mass offered on the altar of the chapel sanctifies everything that involves teaching and learning.

Children’s Park

There is a park for the primary kids in the primary department compound. This was donated by the old Patricians to give opportunities for the primary kids to play and help them develop interaction among themselves. Primary teachers make the best use of this park to engage their students in outdoor activities.

Computer Labs

There are four computer labs to provide sound IT knowledge to students at every section. Besides theoretical knowledge, the students are able to get practical lessons to enhance their IT knowledge. Furthermore, the facilities in the labs motivate our students to get themselves involved in IT related projects and competitions.

Dining Hall

The College Dining Hall is located at the Mathuram’s Square. St. Patrick’s Day morning breakfast, Annual Prize Day lunch, Rector’s Day and Teachers’ Day lunch and any meals associated with the Big Matches and other competitions are organized here. The dining hall is very convenient for this purpose.

Drinking Water

The teachers and students at St. Patrick’s are lucky to have purified water for drinking. The water supplied from a tube well is purified through a system which was donated and maintained by the old students of the College. The drinking water is provided all throughout the school hours without any interruption.

Hostel

The College Hostel, “Home of Love” is one of the oldest facilities provided by the College to the students who come from faraway places to study at the College. The earlier hostel was built by former Rector Rev. Fr. G.A. Francis Joseph and it is located next to the Matthews’ Block. Very recently it was shifted to a diocesan property, St. Paul’s Centre which is adjacent to the east wall of the College. In order to fulfill the growing needs of the hostellers, a new building has been erected within the hostel compound.

Laboratories

The College has been equipped with a number of laboratories: Science Laboratories for junior and senior secondary students, laboratories for the advanced level students such as Biology, Physics, Chemistry, Engineering and Bio System Technology. The teachers and students make the bust use of these laboratories to enhance their practical knowledge in relevant subjects.

Library

The College Library is one of the best school libraries in the Jaffna district. Created by the legendry rector of the College, Rev. Fr. T.M.F. Long and developed by his successors, the College Library houses thousands of books, magazines, journals and manuscripts. The books and journals are classified and easy to access to the readers. The library has a very good seating facilities and enough spaces for personal reading and learning. The old boys continue to donate books and other materials to enhance its standard.

Media Unit

It is one of the most important facilities of the College as it carries all what happen in the College to the outside world. The College Official Facebook and the Website are managed and operated by the Media Unit which consists of the IT learning students of the College. The old boys have donated some modern equipment to enhance the quality of the work of the Media Unit. The Unit continues to provide a great service to the Patrician Community.

Open Air Stage (Mathuram Square)

The College has the facility of an open air stage at the Mathuram Square. It is adjacent to Arumai Block which was used as staff room earlier. Many Annual Prize Day functions had taken place at this open air stage earlier. The daily morning assembly of the Senior Secondary Section takes place here. It is very suitable and convenient to conduct various College functions.

Playground

St. Patrick’s College playground, known as Bishop Deogupillai Stadium is one of the biggest sports arena in the district of Jaffna. The stadium has a pavilion and dressing rooms for players and in operation from 1985. The location of the playground within the College compound itself is really beneficial to the students and teachers. Many schools and private institutes often desire to use our playground to conduct their sports and games.

Sanitation

The Sanitary Facility at the College is really good and maintained well. Each section of the College has got its own sanitary unit. The units are cleaned by workers daily after school.

Score Board

The current cricket score board was erected at the stadium in 1987 by former Rector Rev. Fr. G.A. Francis Joseph. It is a massive score board which is able to present a very visible and clear score details to the spectators at any corner of the playground. Though it is not a digital or electricity powered score board, the work it does to us is still fantastic. We gratefully remember its creator, Rev. Fr. G.A. Francis Joseph.

Security

The major part of the College premises are under the surveillance cameras 24/7. Besides there are security guards at the main entrances of the College to provide security to teachers, student and the College property.

Smart Classrooms

The College has been equipped with more than twenty smart classrooms which provide new technology based learning experience to the students. With the help of the smart classrooms, the teachers are able to improve their efficiency in teaching and provide better learning environment to their students. The College is ever grateful to the generous donors around the globe for their continuous support in establishing smart classrooms at the College.

Sports Pavilion

A new sports pavilion with fine seating facilities on the ground level and balcony and dressing and resting rooms for players has been in use from 2016. The entire cost of this pavilion was sponsored by old Patricians Mr. Charles Alagaratnam and his brothers.

Technology Centre

One of the very recent and huge buildings of the College is the Technology Centre located next to the Long Block. The old Patricians all over the world financially contributed towards the construction. The new building was opened by Former President of Sri Lanka Maithripala Srisena on 19th March 2018.

Tennis Courts

There are two tennis courts in the College. One is located at the Mathuram Square and the other between the Chapel and the Long Block. The former is a hard court made of cement and the latter is a clay court.

Tuck Shop

Tuck shop is one of the new things at St. Patrick’s. It is located along the tennis court in front of the Long Block. Students are lucky to buy stationery items such as note books, pens, pencils, foolscap sheets etc. They can also get school bags, ties, shocks and make photocopies of their study materials.

Turf Wicket

The first Turf Wicket in the North was laid at the College Sports Arena on 29th October 2014 under the distinguished patronage of our Bishop Rt. Rev. Dr. Justin B. Gnanapragasam, along with several dignitaries, Hon. Sanath Jayasuriya and Hon. Muthiah Muralitharan, veteran national cricketers and some special guests. The Sri Lanka Cricket laid the Centre Pitch while the College management completed other task of earth filling, levelling, planting grass and water supply system.

Volleyball Court

The College has also the facility of a volleyball court next to the basketball court. The College Volleyball Team makes the best use of it.

Wells

There are two wells in the College compound to fulfill the need of water supply for teachers and students, sanitation, plants, flower gardens and various other needs.
