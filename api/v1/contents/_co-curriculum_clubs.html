The Social Service League

In order inculcate among the students a spirit of service to the community, the Social Service League organizes Social Service Week every year comprising awareness programmes and Shramadana. The members of this club actively take part in many service projects both within and outside the College under the guidance of their moderator.

The Interact Club

It is a service oriented club. Its members organize many activities such as Blood Donation Campaign, awareness programmes to prevent Dengue fever, conducting traffic diversion continually in the morning and afternoon during school hours etc. Many of the shady and fruit bearing fruits found in the College compound are the result of the hard work of the members of the Interact Club.

The Thaninayagam Muthamil Mantram

The objective of the Muthamil Mantram is to foster interest among students in Tamil Literary works and develop creative skills. To achieve this objective, the Mantram organizes various competitions on essay writing, debate, drama, poetry and oratory. The members also organize Muthamil Vizha every year in which varieties of cultural events such as Naattu Koothu, Villu Paddu, Paddi Mantram, Dramas etc.

The English Union

Being mindful of the importance of English Language, the Union makes its efforts to involve all students to develop their English Language skills. Every year, our students secure a number of places in the competitions held at Divisional, Zonal, District and Provincial Levels in various contests. The Union organizes the Annual English Day Celebration which presents colorful stage performances including Shakespearean Poetic Dramas.

Scout Troop & Cub Scouts

The members of the Scout Troop are very active. They conduct their regular Patrol Meetings with their Troop Leader. The scouts render their services not only at all important College events but also at Church festivals and public functions outside the College. †The Guard of Honour given by the Scout Troops to the Guests at the Inter House Athletic Meet is very smart and grandeur.

Catholic Union & Sacred Heart Confraternity

Maintaining the Catholic atmosphere at the College is our prime concern. The Union and the Confraternity are mainly involved in promoting it. The Union prepares the students to conduct the religious services and devotions on First Fridays, at May Devotions, October Devotions and on special days. The Union also prepares the students for the Holy Masses on special days throughout the academic year, collects clothes and food items to distributes to the needy during lent, Oli Vizha and Christmas carols etc.
